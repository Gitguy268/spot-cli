A black lab who lives in your terminal.

Spot sits in your prompt, gets hungry over the day, and wags when your tests pass. Break the build and he puts his head on your knee. Fix it and he does zoomies.

Get Spot Source on GitHub
brew install gitguy268/tap/spot-cli
Click Spot to pet him.

He watches your tests

One line in your shell config and Spot notices go test, npm test, pytest, cargo test and fifty other test commands. A one-second reaction, then he waits in your scrollback.

A good boy, not a chore

Spot is built to make your day nicer, not to guilt you.

🟢

Reacts to every test run

A wag and a treat for green, comfort for red, and zoomies across the screen when you fix a broken run.

🐶

Lives in your prompt

A tiny U^ᴥ^U next to your prompt shows how he feels. It costs a few milliseconds.

🏠

Has his own room

Run spot to pet him, feed him, play fetch and see his tricks. Tests in other terminals show up there live.

🦴

Never dies

He gets hungry and a bit blue if you're away, then sleeps by the door. A free bowl of kibble every four hours.

✦

Learns tricks

Pass streaks and a strong bond teach him high five, speak, roll over, spin and play sleepy. Twelve badges to earn together.

◐

Fits your terminal

Dark and light themes, 16 colours, no colour at all, and small windows. zsh, bash, fish and PowerShell.

Install

One binary, no daemon, no network.

brew trust gitguy268/tap   # once, for new Homebrew
brew install gitguy268/tap/spot-cli
go install github.com/Gitguy268/spot-cli/cmd/spot@latest
# macOS, Linux and Windows builds:
https://github.com/Gitguy268/spot-cli/releases/latest

Bring him home

Add one line to your shell config, then open a new terminal.

# ~/.zshrc, after your theme
eval "$(spot init zsh --prompt)"
# ~/.bashrc
eval "$(spot init bash --prompt)"
# ~/.config/fish/config.fish
spot init fish --prompt | source
# $PROFILE
Invoke-Expression (& spot init pwsh --prompt | Out-String)

Leave out --prompt to keep your prompt as it is and use $SPOT_FACE yourself, for example in starship or p10k. Not using hooks? spot run -- go test ./... works anywhere and keeps the exit code.
